### CHEM-000548: Durable organic passivation of neutral-pH silicon photocathodes
- **Subfield:** Water splitting, hydrogen production and fuel cells
- **Open:** Preserve charge extraction and hydrogen evolution while preventing corrosion through defects, catalyst boundaries and aging in organic silicon-passivation layers.
